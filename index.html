CASE STUDY 01 · FPT SOFTWARE

AI Marketplace Platform

A place where ideas turn into working, reviewed solutions — and the good ones become reusable for every team.

  1. Anyone posts an idea — a problem worth solving.
  2. Anyone picks it up and works on the task.
  3. They submit the solution with documentation and a video demo.
  4. AI evaluates the submission and returns feedback.
  5. Reviewers add their own feedback on open solutions.
  6. Reviewers mark reusable solutions, so other teams can pick them up.
DESIGNED TOTurn good ideas into working solutions, not slide decks.
DESIGNED TOGive every submission fast, consistent feedback.
DESIGNED TOMake reuse explicit, so work isn't rebuilt twice.

CASE STUDY 02 · AGENTIC AI

AI Digital Coworker

An agent that lives where people already work — Teams and Slack — and resolves requests across Jira, SharePoint and Confluence.

  1. A request arrives in Teams or Slack, in plain language.
  2. The agent plans and decides which enterprise tools it needs.
  3. It acts — working in Jira, and finding knowledge in SharePoint and Confluence.
  4. It closes the loop in the same chat, and hands off to a human when needed.
DESIGNED TOKeep people in their chat tool instead of hopping between systems.
DESIGNED TOResolve requests autonomously, end to end.
DESIGNED TOKeep a human in the loop where it matters.

From Idea to Working System: Notes on Building and Scaling Applied AI

Mathan Sakthivel1,2 1CitiusTech, AI First Engineering · 2Dept. of Data Science and Business Systems, SRM Institute of Science and Technology, Kattankulathur mail2mathans@gmail.com

Abstract

We present an engineer with 14+ years across product, platform and applied AI. Starting from .NET and the MERN stack, the subject progressively specialised in large language models, retrieval-augmented generation and agentic systems. We show that rapid prototyping, paired with deliberate enablement, moves organisations from experimentation to adoption: through the Gen AI Academy at GlobalLogic this approach reached 5,000+ people. Ongoing work includes multi-agent intelligent tutoring systems.

Keywords — GenAI · RAG · multi-agent systems · AI adoption · healthcare · EdTech

1Introduction

The author joins CitiusTech as Engineering Manager — AI in Software Engineering, within the AI First Engineering team. Previously he headed AI, client enablement and adoption at FPT Software, building insurance and aviation prototypes and demoing them directly to clients.

His working method is simple: convert an idea to a running application quickly, using AI-assisted tooling, then make it repeatable for the team.

3Methods

Models: GPT-4o, Claude, Llama, Mistral. Orchestration: LangGraph, AutoGen, CrewAI, n8n. Retrieval: pgvector, FAISS, Chroma, Pinecone. Infrastructure: Azure, AWS, GCP, Docker. Language of record: Python.

Algorithm 1 Idea → System
1: prototype(idea) with AI-assisted tools
2: ground it in real data (RAG)
3: orchestrate agents for the workflow
4: demo it to the client
5: enable the team → adoption
